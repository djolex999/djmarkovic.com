At my first backend internship in 2022, I wrote a forgot-password API. It was a simple thing, and it took me two weeks. Most of that time went into fixing what came back from review, again and again. For a while I couldn't even test it, because the email service only sent mail from a registered domain.

The next year, on another project, I spent a long time on a login bug that turned out to be one missing line.

Now I build features like that in an afternoon, with Claude writing most of the code.

It isn't a fair comparison. Back then I was learning, and a senior developer reviewed everything I wrote. But the review didn't go away. Back then someone else did it for me. Now it's mine, and it's most of the work.
