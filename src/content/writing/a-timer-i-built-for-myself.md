I kept losing focus with too many tabs open. So in September I built [fokus](https://github.com/djolex999/fokus), a small timer for my own desk. You pick a task and 10, 25 or 50 minutes, and it counts down in a little window that stays on top of everything else.

The part I needed most is a shortcut. When a thought shows up mid-task, like replying to an email or checking an invoice, I press it, type one line, hit Enter, and I'm back in the app I was in. The thought is written down, so I don't have to go after it. Later there's a short list to go through: done, schedule or delete.

Some things it doesn't do, on purpose: no notifications, no accounts, no internet, no streaks. Ending a session early is just recorded. There's no red X for it.

I still use it every day.
