My Claude account was approved for Defense Access in Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program. It covers defensive work on systems I own or maintain: vulnerability triage and verification, malware analysis, detection engineering and incident response. Claude's safety checks should interrupt that work less often.

I run a few products with real users, and some of my most useful lessons came from security work, including a fix that opened a new hole and was caught in review before it shipped.

The first thing I'll use the access for is a full security review of pripremi.rs before card payments go live. They're in final testing now.

Same rule as always: only my own systems, and nothing gets called fixed until it's verified. I'll write about what the review finds once it's fixed.
