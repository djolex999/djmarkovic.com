I studied computer science at the Faculty of Natural Sciences in North Mitrovica, from 2019 to 2026. It's a small faculty, far from Belgrade's big technical schools.

<!-- TODO (Djole): what studying there was like, and what was missing -->

Most of what I know I learned outside lectures. In 2022 I started a remote backend internship, building Node.js APIs where senior developers reviewed every pull request. A second backend role followed in 2023, in a Scrum team. Later I started building my own products: pripremi.rs, a lesson-plan generator for Serbian teachers, vir, an open-source tool for coding agents, and serbeval, a benchmark for how language models handle Serbian.

This month I went to my first hackathon, Adapty's First Commit in Belgrade. Many of the teams were students from ETF and RAF. We took 2nd place.
