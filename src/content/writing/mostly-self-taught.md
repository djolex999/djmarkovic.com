I've been thinking lately about where I learned what I know.

I studied computer science at the Faculty of Natural Sciences in North Mitrovica, from 2019 to 2026. Studying there was quiet. There wasn't much practice, and not many people from the industry around.

Living there, you don't get many chances to show yourself in the industry. The IT sector is larger in Belgrade and other bigger cities, and the opportunities that come with living and studying there weren't around me. As a Serb in Kosovo, the scene I could be part of was smaller still.

I'm mostly self-taught. I learned from online courses, and from a few events that were a bit like small hackathons, but not quite. In 2022 I started a remote backend internship, where senior developers reviewed every pull request. I also built my own products: pripremi.rs, vir and serbeval.

The monotony could wear down my will to work. I worked, but not as much as I'm capable of. Whenever I left town, I felt more will and energy for bigger work. Since I moved, that has changed a lot.

Still, through all of it I managed to build something, however small. I didn't stand still.

This month I went to my first hackathon, Adapty's First Commit in Belgrade. I felt like an outsider there, and that was a motive for me. Our team took 2nd place. I wrote about what I built in [My first hackathon](/writing/my-first-hackathon).
